How it works

Three steps, no migration.

Connect your channel manager and the AI reads your past conversations to learn how you write. There is nothing to import and nothing to configure first.

01

One queue for every channel

Airbnb, Vrbo, direct bookings, SMS and email arrive in the same place. Guest and owner threads sit side by side, deduplicated.

02

Replies drawn from your details

The AI answers from the property information you have entered, and cites which document each fact came from. When it does not know, it asks you rather than guessing.

03

You send, or it does

Review and send with one tap, or turn on auto-send and let quiet-hours, privacy and policy checks decide. You can switch between the two per property.

Platform

The rest of the job, in the same place.

Messaging is where most of the day goes, but it is not the whole day. Property facts, turnovers, your team and your notes live alongside the inbox rather than in four other tools.

One record per property

Codes, parking, wifi, quirks and house rules in one place, syncing with your channel manager so the AI and your team read the same thing.

Collaborators with roles

Add and remove co-hosts, cleaners, maintenance and owners, each seeing only what their job needs.

Mobile app and desktop

The same account on a phone and in a browser, so approving a draft from a car park works as well as from a desk.

Connect any platform

Built for Guesty, with direct connections for the other channels you already sell on.

It learns as it goes

Every edit you make to a draft teaches it how you write. The replies need less correction the longer you use it.

Support inside 48 hours

A real support team with a 48-hour response commitment, included at every plan size.

Security

Your guests' data stays yours.

Send controls you set

Every reply either waits for a person or passes the quiet-hours, privacy and policy checks you configure. Auto-send is off until you turn it on.

Answers tied to sources

Replies are drawn from the property details you entered, each answer citing where it came from. The AI asks you rather than inventing a fact.

Guest data stays out of training

Personal details are redacted before anything reaches the model and are never used to train it. The privacy policy sets out what we hold and why.
