WE'RE HIRING

Help us automate the work, so we can show up for our guests

Join Regent Properties and help build the future of technology-driven hospitality.

Property Operations & Automation Manager
Full-Time Remote + Travel Contract

Part hospitality, part AI builder. Hands-on operations mixed with AI-powered automation work. No traditional experience required — just curiosity and ownership.

About This Role

One moment you might be helping a guest with a check-in issue. The next, you could be coordinating an HVAC technician, building an AI workflow, updating a spreadsheet, purchasing furniture for a new property, or figuring out how to prevent an operational problem from happening again. We'll provide hands-on training — what matters most is that you're resourceful, communicate extremely well, learn quickly, and take ownership of problems.

What You'll Do

  • Respond quickly to guest questions with empathy, enthusiasm, and excellent service
  • Manage day-to-day operations across our properties
  • Build and improve AI-powered automations and operational workflows
  • Use ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI tools to solve real business problems
  • Coordinate cleaners, contractors, technicians, vendors, and repairs from start to finish
  • Track projects, purchases, expenses, maintenance, and operational issues
  • Work comfortably in Excel, Google Sheets, Docs, and other SaaS platforms
  • Identify repetitive work and figure out how technology can eliminate it
  • Help launch new properties from an empty space to a fully operational guest experience
  • Travel when needed to oversee new-property setup, installations, and repairs
  • Design and furnish properties — sourcing furniture, electronics, linens, and supplies
  • Own problems through completion instead of simply passing them to someone else

Who We're Looking For

  • An exceptional written and verbal communicator
  • Naturally responsive and frequently available by phone
  • Provide genuinely great customer service, even when someone is frustrated
  • Have strong attention to detail and notice things other people miss
  • Comfortable juggling multiple priorities without losing track of them
  • A self-starter who doesn't need to be told every next step
  • Comfortable learning new technology and software quickly
  • Think in systems — when something happens repeatedly, you improve the process
  • Comfortable working independently and making good judgment calls
  • Want responsibility and room to grow, not just tasks to complete

What Stands Out

You don't need to be a software engineer. But we'd especially value if you've:

  • Written functional Python, JavaScript, or another scripting language
  • Built an AI agent, automation, chatbot, or workflow — even a personal project
  • Used APIs, Zapier, Make, n8n, or similar automation tools
  • Have a technical, engineering, data, or analytical background
  • Have a bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience
  • Have experimented with AI beyond asking ChatGPT basic questions
  • Can look at a repetitive manual process and start thinking about how to automate it

Side projects count. Hobby projects count. Things you built because you were curious absolutely count. We care much more about what you can figure out than whether every skill came from a previous job.

Schedule & Growth

Hospitality happens throughout the day. Much of the work is quick guest messages and timely operational decisions, mixed with longer stretches of project and automation work. You should be comfortable with a flexible schedule and being responsive during your assigned coverage periods, including occasional evenings or weekends. Travel of up to 20% may be required.

This begins as a six-month contract, but we're looking for someone who can grow with Regent. Strong performers may be offered a permanent full-time position with increased compensation and benefits. For eligible roles, Regent may also provide H-1B sponsorship.

About Us

Technology-driven hospitality, built by people who use AI daily

BookWithRegent.com is a technology-driven property management and hospitality company. We manage properties for owners, operate our own portfolio, and build AI-powered systems that automate as much of the day-to-day work of property management as possible.

Our philosophy is simple: technology should handle the repetitive work so people can focus on the things that actually require people. That means automating routine communication, operational workflows, reporting, and task management — while still delivering genuinely great hospitality when a guest needs a human. We're growing, and looking for someone who wants to help us build that future.
