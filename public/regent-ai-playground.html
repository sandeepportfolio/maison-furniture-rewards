Is this the actual Regent application?

No. This is an educational, scripted simulation of the guest-messaging workflow. The examples are prewritten, not generated by a live AI model. It is not a pixel-for-pixel replica of the app, and no messages are sent.

What do I provide to Regent?

Hosts and operators provide and review property details. In the live application, Regent uses the relevant conversation and property context to draft guest replies. Accurate, current information matters.

Does Regent automatically send every reply?

No. Hosts can review and send replies themselves. Optional auto-send depends on configured controls and delivery checks. A reply that needs review remains a suggestion, not a delivered message. This demo simplifies those checks for teaching.

Can it approve exceptions or fix a maintenance issue?

A message draft does not approve a late checkout, authorize spending, or repair a broken appliance. People still need to make decisions and carry out operational work. Always verify important information before sending.

Is any personal information collected here?

This demo has no forms, analytics, live AI calls, or guest-service connections. Your choices and draft edits stay in this page’s memory and reset when you reload. Do not enter real guest information.
