The takeaway

Three things to notice before you trust any inbox AI.

Grounded, not guessed.

Every fact in a draft traces back to a line in your property record. Change the record and the draft follows. No record, no claim.

Unknowns stay visible.

When the record answers half the question, the draft says which half. It never invents a high chair to make the reply sound complete.

The decision stays yours.

Routine answers can pass the checks and send. Exceptions, like a late checkout, are routed to you. A draft is never an approval.

Helpful AI. Not a magic wand.
