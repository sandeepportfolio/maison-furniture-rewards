Questions

Plain answers.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. If you can describe what you want in a text message, that’s enough. We handle every technical decision from start to finish, and we explain things in plain English when you’d like to know.

What does $100 a month get me?

A sophisticated, custom-designed business website with your own dedicated admin portal. Websites start at $100 a month. Larger sites with more features are priced to match, and you’ll know the number before we build anything.

How do we stay in touch while you build?

However you prefer: text, email or a quick phone call. We take detailed notes on every conversation, so you never have to repeat yourself.

How often will I see progress?

As often as you like. Some owners want a demo every week, and others would rather just see the finished product. It’s your call, and you can change your mind along the way.

How long does a custom app take?

Within a month, and usually sooner, depending on how technically complex it is.

Where can AI actually help my business?

Anywhere someone repeats the same steps: returning missed calls, sending invoices and reminders, booking and rescheduling, copying information between systems. We’ll look at your week with you and show you what’s worth automating.
